The Dance of the Twelve Moons Created by Arthur S Junaluska The Dance tells the story of the beginning of Nature, Life and Beyond. Prologue Out of the hallowed, limitless space of time, playing hide and seek from behind the moving clouds, the chanting echoes of the past emerge forth once again, and the wind is its voice. Listen: The first beat of the sacred hollow drum resounds through every fibre, even now the whispering of the wind, you can here the faint moccasined footsteps, of the spirited dancers.
