As the Four Winds come moaning in with its huge fan penetrating the surroundings, it seems to chant a prayer to warn us, to be ever alert. For now the breeze has a tingling effect as nature changes, and the dew is moulded into frost, giving some of Mother Earth's beings a coated radiance. The leaves begin to fall, and nature undergoes a great change. The medicine men have made contact with the Great-Mystery, and have been warned to cleanse their bodies, and drive out all evil spirits that surround and disturb their environment.
