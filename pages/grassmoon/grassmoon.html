With the coming of the Grass Moon, the Sun Father smiles and the unfamiliar designs begin to disappear into the receding shadows of the day. New events begin to take form, nature beings begin to live and spring up, a new awakening. Once again there is no unrest, and there shall be no hunger, and in particular, the fish that are caught in the nets, still leap and squirm, trying to free themselves for a new rebirth.
