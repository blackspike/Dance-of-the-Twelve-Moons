The Gods and the Spirit Winds have watched that we do not stray from the trail of beauty and romance, as signified by the blooming of the Rose, the Four Winds have stolen into our midst ever so gently, to give us peace. New designs appear in the sky, strange noises are heard and repeated, but all is a thing of beauty Dew-drops fall to sprinkle and cleanse us, and the Sun-Father drinks it up, and thus illuminates the sky with his great Arch-Way, the Rainbow.
